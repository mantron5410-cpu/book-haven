Why Book Haven exists

Our Mission

Our mission is to raise money through fun and exciting raffles while helping the librarian keep the library clean, organized, and welcoming for all students. We want to support our librarian, encourage students to participate, and make a positive difference in our school community. We strive to make the library a better place while providing great service to our school community.

✶ ✶ ✶

Our Vision

Our vision is to create a fun and successful business that sells raffle tickets to raise money while helping keep the school library organized, clean, and welcoming.
